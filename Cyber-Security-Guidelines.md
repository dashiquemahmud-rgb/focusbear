I understand that remote work creates security risks such as phishing, malicious downloads, stolen passwords and unauthorised access to unattended devices. At Focus Bear, protecting company and user information needs to be part of my daily routine.

I need to review my current account and device settings before confirming that they meet these expectations. I will use long, unique passwords stored in an approved password manager and enable two-factor authentication wherever available. This adds protection if a password is compromised. I will also keep software updated and configure my computer and phone to lock automatically after a few minutes, requiring a password or biometric login.

If I receive an unexpected link or attachment, I will verify the request through a trusted channel before opening it. I will never share verification codes or approve unexpected login prompts. If I suspect a breach, I will promptly notify my supervisor or designated security contact, preserve relevant evidence and follow their instructions.

My new security habit will be locking my computer every time I leave my desk, even briefly. This simple action helps prevent others from accessing confidential information through my signed-in accounts.
