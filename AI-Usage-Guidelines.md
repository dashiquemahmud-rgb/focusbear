AI can help me brainstorm test cases, organise notes and improve the clarity of bug reports during my Focus Bear internship. However, I remain responsible for checking its output. I will rely on my own judgement when interpreting test results, evaluating user experiences and deciding whether an issue has been resolved.

For a practice task, I used AI to generate a checklist for testing whether a focus session synchronises between devices. The initial suggestions were to start a session, check the second device and confirm that the session details match. On review, this needed more detail: both devices should use the same account, connectivity should be checked, and expected behaviour should be confirmed against product requirements. This showed me that AI suggestions need refinement before use.

I will never enter passwords, access tokens or identifiable user records into AI prompts. I will also keep confidential company material out of unapproved tools. Instead, I will use fictional examples and only the minimum information necessary.

My best practice is to treat AI output as a draft: verify claims against approved documentation, test suggested steps and report only results I have actually observed.
