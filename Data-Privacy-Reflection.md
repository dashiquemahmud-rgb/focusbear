# Data Privacy and Confidentiality Reflection

After reviewing Focus Bear’s privacy policy, I learned that the company handles sensitive information, including users’ contact details, habits, device information, subscription records, and potentially health or religious information. Internal documents, source code, passwords and unreleased product information must also remain confidential.

In my daily work, I will access only the information required for my task. I will use approved systems, strong passwords and multi-factor authentication, lock unattended devices, and use anonymised data during QA activities whenever possible. Before sharing a file or screenshot, I will check the recipient, remove unnecessary personal details and restrict access.

Sensitive information should never be stored in personal email, unapproved cloud services or public links. Unnecessary copies should be deleted securely. Common mistakes include sending files to the wrong person, exposing data in screenshots and leaving devices unlocked. If I suspect a breach, I will limit further exposure and report it immediately to my supervisor or Focus Bear’s privacy contact.

My security habit will be to complete a **recipient, content and permission check** before sharing information. I Saved Data-Privacy-Reflection.md on GitHub
